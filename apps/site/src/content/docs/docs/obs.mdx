Vellum runs in OBS as a **browser source** laid over your webcam. OBS's **Virtual Camera** hands the finished picture to Discord as your camera. The Vellum script for OBS builds all of that for you.

**You need:** [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) 28 or newer on Windows, macOS or Linux. Nothing else to install: OBS runs the script itself.

## 1. Get your link

Open [the app](/app/), fill in your character, and press **Copy share link**. The link carries your whole character, which is how it gets into OBS (OBS has its own browser storage, separate from your normal browser).

GMs can skip this step. With no link, the script uses the player screen; paste `https://vellumhud.com/app/#/daggerheart/gm` for the GM screen.

## 2. Load the script

1. Download [`vellum.lua`](/downloads/vellum.lua) and keep it somewhere it won't move, such as your Documents folder. OBS loads it from there every time it starts.
2. In OBS, open **Tools → Scripts**, press **+**, and pick `vellum.lua`.

## 3. Set up Vellum

With **vellum.lua** selected in the Scripts window:

1. Paste your link into **Your Vellum link**.
2. Pick your webcam under **Camera**. If it isn't listed, plug it in and press **Refresh camera list**.
3. Press **Set up Vellum**.

The script makes a scene called **Vellum** with your webcam underneath and the HUD on top, both fitted to your canvas, switches to it, and starts the Virtual Camera. It never touches your other scenes, and pressing it again updates the same scene.

The first time the Virtual Camera starts:

- **macOS:** approve the OBS system extension in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
- **Windows:** the driver comes with OBS; there's normally no prompt.
- **Linux:** load the `v4l2loopback` kernel module so the camera device exists.

## 4. Pick the camera in Discord

In Discord, open **Settings → Voice & Video → Camera** and choose **OBS Virtual Camera**. That's it: your face, with your stats on it.

## During play

- **Change values** with [hotkeys](/docs/control/). The same script adds them: open **Settings → Hotkeys**, search for "Vellum", and bind keys. Or right-click **Vellum HUD**, choose **Interact**, and click pips on the HUD.
- **Changed your character in the app?** Copy the share link again and paste it into **Your Vellum link**. The HUD reloads with it straight away; you don't need to press Set up again.
- **Next session:** OBS remembers the scene and the script. Open OBS and press **Start Virtual Camera**.

## Face tracking

If Vellum draws the camera itself so the nameplate can follow your face, choose **Vellum draws the camera (face tracking)** under **Camera** and press **Set up Vellum** again. The script hides its own webcam source so the two don't fight over the camera. OBS also has to start with `--enable-media-stream`; see [Face tracking](/docs/face-tracking/).

## Setting it up by hand

If you'd rather not run a script, the setup is three sources' worth of clicks:

1. **Sources → + → Browser**. Paste your link as the URL and set the size to **1920 × 1080**. Other sizes work, because the HUD scales to fit, but 1920 × 1080 is sharpest.
2. **Sources → + → Video Capture Device**, pick your webcam, and drag it **below** the browser source.
3. **Start Virtual Camera**, then choose **OBS Virtual Camera** in Discord.

For hotkeys you still need the script, but you can load it and never press Set up.

## Why a script

OBS has a few ways to extend it, and we picked the one that asks least of you:

- **Scripts** (Lua) run inside OBS on every OS with nothing extra to install. They can build scenes, start the Virtual Camera, and register hotkeys, and they talk to the browser source directly, with no network or password. That's everything Vellum needs.
- **Plugins** are compiled native code. They'd need a separate installer per OS and signing on macOS, for nothing a script can't already do.
- **Python scripts** work the same way but need Python installed and pointed at in OBS. Lua comes with OBS.
- **Scene collection files** can carry a browser source, but not your webcam, which is different on every machine, and importing one switches your whole collection.
- **Custom browser docks** show a web page inside OBS, but they keep separate storage from browser sources, so they couldn't carry your character into the HUD.
- **The OBS WebSocket** lets outside apps control OBS, but it needs a password set up, and Vellum's hotkeys don't need it.
