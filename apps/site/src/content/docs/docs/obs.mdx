The [Vellum script](/docs/obs-script/) does this for you. To do it yourself in OBS:

1. **Sources → + → Browser.** Paste your link as the URL and set the size to **1920 × 1080**.
2. **Sources → + → Video Capture Device.** Pick your webcam and drag it **below** the browser source.
3. **Start Virtual Camera.** On macOS, allow the OBS system extension in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. On Linux, load `v4l2loopback`.
4. **In Discord,** open **Settings → Voice & Video → Camera** and choose **OBS Virtual Camera**.

Hotkeys still come from the script: load it and skip **Set up Vellum**.

**Changed your character?** Copy the link again and paste it as the browser source's URL.
