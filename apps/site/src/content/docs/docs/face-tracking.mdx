---
title: Face tracking
description: Anchor HUD widgets to your face with a small detector that runs on your machine.
---

Face tracking lets widgets follow your face instead of sitting in a fixed spot: your conditions ride above your head, your nameplate sits under your chin. It is **off by default**, and every frame works without it.

## What it does and doesn't do

- It finds a box around your face. That's all.
- It doesn't read expressions or recognise who you are.
- It never changes your stats. It only moves widgets.
- It runs on your machine. Camera frames are never stored or sent.

The detector (about 230 KB, plus its runtime) ships with the app, so turning tracking on contacts no server.

## Turning it on

With tracking on, Vellum draws your webcam itself, underneath the HUD, so the widgets and your face stay in sync.

1. **Start OBS with camera access for browser sources.** Add `--enable-media-stream` to how you launch OBS:
   - **Windows:** edit the OBS shortcut's **Target** and add ` --enable-media-stream` after the closing quote.
   - **macOS:** run `open -a OBS --args --enable-media-stream` in Terminal.
   - **Linux:** run `obs --enable-media-stream`, or add it to the `Exec=` line of your OBS desktop file.
2. **Turn it on in setup.** Under **Camera and face tracking**, tick **Draw my camera in Vellum**, then **Widgets follow my face**.
3. **Copy your link again** and paste it into the Vellum browser source, so OBS gets the same settings.
4. **Hide OBS's Video Capture Device.** Vellum carries your camera now, and most webcams can only be opened by one app at a time.

If the camera won't open, the setup panel tells you why and shows the fix. Tracking settings travel in the link as `camera=1` and `face=1`, and `mirror=0` turns off the selfie flip.

## For frame makers

Today the nameplate follows your chin and the conditions follow the top of your head, in every frame, and both slide home when your face leaves the picture. Per-widget face anchors (`face-top`, `face-bottom`, `face-left`, `face-right`, each with a fixed `fallback`) arrive with the frame creator.
