The [Frame Library](/library/) is two folders in the Vellum repository: `official/` and `community/`. Community frames arrive by pull request. Because frames are data, never code, the automated checks and a quick review are the whole process.

## Before you submit

- Your frame passes [the two rules](/docs/frames/rules/). The creator and the tests both check.
- You made it, or you have the right to share it. Don't include art or text from a published game book.
- It has a clear name and a one-line description.

## How to submit

1. Export your frame as a file, or write it by hand.
2. Fork [the repository](https://github.com/alxjrvs/vellum) and add the file to the community folder for its game.
3. Open a pull request. Say which game it's for and attach a screenshot at 640 × 360.

The checks validate the file against the frame schema and the two rules. A maintainer reviews the rest. Once merged, your frame ships in the next build of the app, on the Community shelf, labelled unofficial and credited to you.

## What we won't merge

Frames that break the rules, that copy a publisher's trade dress or art, or that use names or text you don't have rights to.
