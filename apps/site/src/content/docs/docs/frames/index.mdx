A **frame** is your HUD: what it shows (a player's stats or the GM's Fear), its colors, where each widget sits, and whether each stat draws as a bar or as chits. Your character's numbers come from your game, not the frame, so switching frames never changes your sheet.

<Aside type="note" title="Today">
  Daggerheart has three official frames: Daggerheart – Player, Daggerheart – GM, and Ledger (a
  player frame). Pick one on the setup screen, or [make your own](/docs/frames/make/). The Community
  shelf is open for pull requests.
</Aside>

## Where frames come from

The picker has three shelves for each game:

- **Official:** made by the Vellum project.
- **Community:** made by players and merged into the [Frame Library](/library/). Labelled unofficial.
- **My frames:** frames you made or imported. They live only on your machine.

## Every frame has an address

Each frame lives at its own address, `/app/frames/<id>/`, and your share link points there. Paste the new link into your OBS browser source and OBS uses that frame. Library frames ship inside the app, so loading one never contacts a server. A [hotkey](/docs/control/) can also step to the next frame of the same kind mid-session.

Your own frames, which aren't in the Library, are packed into the link too. That makes the link longer but keeps it working with no server.

## Bars or chits

Each frame decides, stat by stat, whether a track draws as one continuous **bar** or as separate **chits**. The same HP can be a bar in one frame and chits in another. If you want a different style, pick a frame that uses it, or [make your own](/docs/frames/make/).
