---
title: Quick start
description: Get your character HUD onto your own Discord camera in a few minutes.
---

Vellum draws your character HUD, OBS lays it over your webcam, and OBS's Virtual Camera hands the finished picture to Discord as your camera. Each player sets this up once, on their own machine.

**You need:** [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) 28 or newer, the Discord desktop app, and a webcam.

## 1. Open Vellum

Open [the app](/app/). It starts on a picker: choose your game (Daggerheart) and your screen, **Player** or **GM**. Each screen has its own address:

- Player: `https://vellumhud.com/app/#/daggerheart/player`
- GM: `https://vellumhud.com/app/#/daggerheart/gm`

The GM screen needs no character, so GMs can skip to step 3.

## 2. Fill your sheet and copy your link

Fill in **Character details** with the numbers from your sheet, then click **Copy share link**. The link carries your whole character in the address. This is how your sheet gets into OBS, which has its own separate browser storage.

## 3. Put it in OBS

1. Download [`vellum.lua`](/downloads/vellum.lua), the Vellum script for OBS.
2. In OBS, open **Tools → Scripts**, press **+** and pick the file.
3. Paste your share link into **Your Vellum link**, pick your webcam under **Camera**, and press **Set up Vellum**.

The script builds a **Vellum** scene with your webcam under the HUD and starts the Virtual Camera. Approve the one-time system prompt if your OS shows one. Prefer to do it by hand? See [OBS setup](/docs/obs/).

## 4. Go on camera

In Discord, open **Settings → Voice & Video → Camera** and choose **OBS Virtual Camera**.

That's it: your face, with your stats on it. Change a value with a [hotkey](/docs/control/), or by clicking the HUD in OBS (right-click the source, **Interact**). Everything you change is saved and survives a reload.

:::tip[Let the setup panel drive]
The first time you open the app, a small setup panel walks you through these steps. Inside OBS it ends with a self-test: click one HP pip and confirm it moved on your camera.
:::
