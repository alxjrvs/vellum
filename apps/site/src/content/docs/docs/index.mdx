You need [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) 28 or newer, the Discord desktop app, and a webcam. Each player does this once.

1. **Copy your link.** Open [the app](/app/), fill in your character, and press **Copy share link**. GMs pick the **Daggerheart – GM** frame and press **Copy GM screen link**.
2. **Load the script.** Download [`vellum.lua`](/downloads/vellum.lua). In OBS, open **Tools → Scripts**, press **+**, and pick it.
3. **Set up.** Paste your link into **Your Vellum link**, pick your webcam, and press **Set up Vellum**. Approve the system prompt if one appears.
4. **Go on camera.** In Discord, open **Settings → Voice & Video → Camera** and choose **OBS Virtual Camera**.

Next time, just open OBS and press **Start Virtual Camera**.
