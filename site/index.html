The shift

Off the tab, onto the table.

In a remote session, everyone's stats live in a character-sheet tab off-camera: private, invisible, and a tab-switch away. Vellum puts them on the webcam feed itself. OBS renders the HUD over your camera, the virtual camera carries it into the call, and your character state becomes information the whole table shares.

Built like a good tool.

Local-only

Nothing leaves your machine

No account, no server, no telemetry. Vellum runs from a static file and writes to localStorage. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to phone home to.

Composable

Daggerheart in the box, your system next

Systems, scenes, and frames are config, not core code. Daggerheart ships as the reference; the platform exists so you can teach it yours.

Made for the call

Readable on a tiny tile

Designed for a 4–6 person Discord call at 480p. Every label stays readable at thumbnail size, on every frame.

For frame makers

Two rules every frame keeps.

A frame can change colors, faces, and whether a stat draws as a bar or as chits. It can't break these two rules, and Vellum checks them.

Labels are always readable

At least 2.1% of the frame's width: 40px on the 1920 canvas, about 13px on a 640×360 Discord tile. At least 7:1 contrast, always on a solid plate, never straight on video.

Everything reads outside-in

Each widget reads from the nearest frame edge toward your face. Left side: label, value, bar. Right side, mirrored: bar, value, label.

For system authors

Compose it.

A system declares its resources, conditions, and character schema. It offers scenes: URL-addressable HUDs, one per role, each the target of an OBS browser source. Scenes compose widgets: tracks, badges, and nameplates bound to your config. A frame skins them.

Add your system as one folder: config plus a few components, no core edits.

System → Scenes → Widgets → Frame 
// frames/daggerheart.ts — illustrative
export const daggerheart: ThemeConfig = {
  id: "daggerheart",
  colors: { plate: "#1b140b", nameplate: "#f3e6c4", … },
  tracks: {
    hp: "chits",
    stress: "bar",
    hope: "chits",
    armor: "chits",
    fear: "chits",
  },
};

Quick start

Four steps to the table.

  1. Step 1

    Open Vellum

    No install. Open the app in your browser, or download the static bundle to run fully offline. Nothing you enter is uploaded.

  2. Step 2

    Build your character

    Fill in the character form, then hit Copy share link. Your whole sheet packs into a URL.

  3. Step 3

    Drop it into OBS

    Import the Vellum scene collection on macOS, Windows, or Linux, or add a browser source pointed at your share link.

  4. Step 4

    Go on camera

    Put your webcam under the HUD, start the OBS Virtual Camera, and pick it in Discord. Every value persists across a reload.
